Janoš Vidali is a researcher at the Institute of Mathematics, Physics and Mechanics and an assistant professor of computational mathematics at the University of Ljubljana, Slovenia. His research interests mainly lie in algebraic combinatorics, specifically on distance-regular graphs and related structures such as association schemes. He has authored software aimed at computations and mathematical data access, both in the narrower research field and more widely in graph theory.
